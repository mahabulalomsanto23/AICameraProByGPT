# Models and licenses

- Current image enhancement is an original deterministic Kotlin bitmap processor. It runs on-device; photos are not uploaded.
- ML Kit Selfie Segmentation is listed as a dependency for planned portrait segmentation but is not yet invoked by the current UI.
- CameraX and AndroidX dependencies use their respective open-source licenses. Google ML Kit terms apply to its SDK.
- No neural upscaling, denoising or scene-classification model is bundled in this initial foundation.
